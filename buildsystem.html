The problem

Claude Code is powerful. It's also chaos without a system.

Every new session starts from scratch. Claude doesn't know your app, your decisions, or what broke yesterday. You spend the first 20 minutes catching it up — and then it drifts anyway.

Context collapse

New session, Claude knows nothing. Your app, your stack, your decisions — gone. 20 minutes gone before you've written a line.

Architecture drift

Claude makes a decision in session 3 that contradicts session 1. Nobody catches it. The codebase becomes a mess.

No clear sequence

What do you build first? What comes after? Without a phase structure, you're guessing every session.

3am fires

Something breaks. You don't know why. Claude doesn't know why. You're debugging at midnight with no context and no system.
