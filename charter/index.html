Features

PDF import, live links

Built-in planners

How it works

From PDF to living notebook in seconds.

01

Import your planner

Tap the + button and import any PDF planner. Charter scans the document, detects every hyperlink zone, and imports all pages instantly — even 700-page planners.

02

Write on every page

Each page becomes a handwriting canvas with your PDF as the background. Write directly onto your planner with Apple Pencil.

03

Tap to navigate

Every link zone from the original PDF is active. Tap monthly overview to jump to a specific week. Navigation works exactly as designed.

04

Sync everywhere

Notebooks sync automatically via iCloud. Start on one iPad, continue on another. Always up to date.
