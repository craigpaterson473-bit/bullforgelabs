Stop losing money on paperwork.

Features

Everything a sole trader needs. Nothing they don’t.

Job costing

Add labour, materials, and line items to every job. See your margin at a glance before the job’s done.

Quotes & invoices

Send professional PDF invoices and quotes straight from site. Branded. Clients can’t ignore them.

Receipt scanning

Point your camera at a receipt. Hoist reads it with OCR and logs it against the job. No more stuffed pockets.

Mileage tracking

Log every site visit with HMRC-approved rates. Automatic running totals. Come tax time, it’s all there.

HMRC-ready exports

Export expenses, mileage and job income as a clean CSV. Hand it to your accountant or file it yourself.

Client management

All your clients and their job history in one place. See what’s outstanding, what’s paid, who to chase.
