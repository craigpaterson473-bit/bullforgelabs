Why Trot

Everything a beginner runner needs.

Run/walk intervals

Proven interval training that builds stamina gradually. No burnout, no injury — just steady progress every session.

Voice coaching

Audio cues tell you exactly when to run and when to walk. No need to look at your phone.

Apple Watch

See your countdown, current interval, and controls on your wrist. Leave your phone in your pocket.

Apple Health

Every run syncs to Apple Health automatically — calories, distance, heart rate, and workout history.

Progress tracking

Watch your progress grow session by session. Every run logged, every improvement visible.

Share your runs

Celebrate every session with a shareable card. Show the world you’re on your way to your first 5K.

The Programme

9 weeks. 3 runs a week. One big finish.

Start with gentle 60-second runs separated by walking recovery. Each week builds on the last until you’re running a full 5K without stopping.
