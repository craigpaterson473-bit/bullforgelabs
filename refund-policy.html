Refund Policy

BullForge Labs Ltd  ·  Last updated: May 2026

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, email us at admin@bullforgelabs.com and we'll sort it out.

Digital Downloads

Digital products (document templates, guides, toolkits) are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of purchase, provided you have not downloaded the files.

Once the files have been downloaded, we are unable to offer a refund as the product has been delivered in full. This is in line with UK consumer rights law for digital content that has been accessed.

If you experience a technical issue with your download or the files are not as described, please contact us and we will resolve it promptly — including a full refund if appropriate.

iOS App Purchases

In-app purchases made through our iOS apps (Trot, Hoist, and others) are processed by Apple. Refund requests for App Store purchases must be made directly through Apple:

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Sign in with your Apple ID
  • Find the purchase and select "Request a refund"

BullForge Labs Ltd does not process refunds for App Store transactions — Apple handles these under their own refund policy.

How to Request a Refund

For digital download refunds, email us at admin@bullforgelabs.com with:

  • Your order number (from your Lemon Squeezy receipt email)
  • The email address used at purchase
  • A brief reason for the refund request

We aim to respond within 1 business day and process approved refunds within 5–10 business days depending on your payment method.

Your Rights

If you are based in the United Kingdom or European Union, you have additional rights under consumer protection law. Nothing in this policy affects those statutory rights. For UK customers, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 applies to digital content purchases.
