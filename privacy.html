Privacy Policy

BullForge Labs Ltd  ·  Last updated: May 2026

BullForge Labs Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is an independent digital studio registered in England and Wales. This privacy policy explains how we handle information across our products and services.

Our iOS Apps

Our iOS apps (Trot, Hoist, and others) do not collect personal data. They store data locally on your device and sync via your personal iCloud account where applicable. We have no access to that data. Each app has its own privacy policy linked from the App Store listing.

  • Trot Privacy Policy
  • Hoist Privacy Policy

Digital Products

When you purchase a digital product from us via Lemon Squeezy, your payment and billing information is handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy as the merchant of record. We receive your email address to deliver the product and for purchase records. We do not store card details.

VibeFix

When you submit a codebase for a VibeFix scan, you share files or a GitHub link with us. We use this solely to complete the audit. We do not retain your code after the report is delivered, and we do not share it with third parties.

Contact and Email

If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide to respond to your query. We do not add you to any marketing list without your explicit consent.

Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. We do not run advertising.

Your Rights

If you are based in the UK or EU, you have rights under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us at admin@bullforgelabs.com to exercise these rights.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will revise the date at the top of this page.
