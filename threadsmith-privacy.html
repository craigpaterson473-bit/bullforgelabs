Privacy Policy — Threadsmith

BullForge Labs Ltd  ·  Last updated: May 2026

Threadsmith is developed by BullForge Labs Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), registered in England and Wales. This policy explains what data Threadsmith collects, how it is used, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

Threadsmith collects the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address — collected at sign-up and used solely for account authentication via Supabase.
  • Product details you enter — names, descriptions, and keywords you provide to power the thread scanning and reply drafting features.
  • Usage data — AI token usage and scan history, used to operate the service and display your activity within the app.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA or similar)
  • No location data
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No payment information (handled entirely by Apple StoreKit)

Your Claude API key

Threadsmith uses a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) model. Your Claude API key is stored securely in your device's keychain and is never transmitted to our servers. When the app calls the Claude API, it does so directly from your device using your own key. We do not have access to your API key at any time.

How we use your data

  • Email — account authentication only. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Product data — to power the scanning and reply drafting features. We do not share this with third parties.
  • Usage data — to operate and improve the service.

Third-party services

Threadsmith uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Data is processed in the EU and US under Supabase's data processing agreement. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Anthropic — Claude API, called directly from your device using your own API key. We are not a party to those requests. See anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Apple StoreKit — subscription billing. All payment data is held by Apple. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Accounts with no activity for 12 months will be deleted along with all associated data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app.

Your rights under UK GDPR

If you are based in the United Kingdom or European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right to deletion — delete your account and all data at any time in-app, or contact us to request deletion.
  • Right to access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to portability — request an export of your data by contacting us.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to object — object to processing in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@bullforgelabs.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Data security

Account data is stored securely via Supabase with encryption at rest and in transit. Your API key is stored in your device keychain and never leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify users via the app where appropriate.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email admin@bullforgelabs.com.
